about.md

I graduated with a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology from the University of Mumbai in 2023. After completing my degree, I pursued a 9-month Full Stack Java Developer program at SPRK Classes, where I strengthened my practical knowledge of software development, object-oriented programming, and modern web technologies.

To gain real-world experience, I worked on freelance web development projects, designing and developing responsive websites for clients. This hands-on experience enhanced my understanding of the complete software development lifecycle, from planning and development to deployment.

In 2025, I joined Tata Memorial Centre as an Assistant Data Manager and, through consistent performance, was promoted to Junior Data Manager and later to my current role as a Research Coordinator. I now work in the Division of Molecular Epidemiology & Population Genetics, where I contribute to large-scale biomedical research by developing data analysis pipelines, managing genomic datasets, and supporting bioinformatics and epidemiological studies.

My technical expertise includes Python, Java, C/C++, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Bootstrap, SQL, and R, with a growing focus on bioinformatics, data analysis, automation, and AI-powered solutions. I enjoy solving complex problems, building efficient tools, and continuously expanding my knowledge of emerging technologies.

I am passionate about combining software engineering, data science, and biomedical research to create impactful, scalable solutions and am always eager to take on new challenges that contribute to both scientific research and technological innovation.
